## Capture an application

![Capture config](/assets/images/launcher/Capture_options.png)

To capture an application select

- the **Capture** mode,
- the **Target**, the application you want to capture,
- the **API** the application uses,
- the **Output path** and
- optionally adjust configuration options as needed.

Click on the green **Start Capture** button and the capture is on it's way, the launcher is taking care of all required steps behind the scenes.

## Playback a stream

![Playback config](/assets/images/launcher/Playback_options.png)

To playback a stream simply

- select the **Playback** mode,
- open the **Stream** and
- optionally adjust configuration options as needed.

Click on the **Start Playback** button and the playback will start. Additionally you can also also view information about the stream (left sidebar: **Metadata**) or dump resources from the stream (left sidebar: **Resource Dump**).

## Subcapture a stream

![Subcapture config](/assets/images/launcher/Subcapture_options.png)

To subcapture a stream

- select the **Subcapture** mode,
- open the **Stream**,
- choose the **Output path** and
- specify the frame range of the new subcapture.

Click on the **Start Subcapture** button and the Launcher will take care of the required steps.
